# Nibble, Sip, Repeat / The Weekend Secret Club

## Positioning
We find what you didn't know existed. You get to experience it. A weekly local discovery, not an endless directory. Human connection, slow moments and host stories are the differentiator.

## Visitor experience
1. See a single-screen invitation led by surprise and human connection.
2. Open **We Found This** to glimpse the editorial tone. Current examples are **concepts only**, deliberately not named or sold as real inventory.
3. Open **Plan My Weekend** for starting location, timing, company, distance and desired feeling. AI creates a possibility note, not a bookable itinerary.
4. Use **Surprise Me** for unexpected concept teases.
5. Join the opt-in form to get notified once the first verified finds are ready.

## What makes it a real product
Create an evidence-based collection first. Start with **12–15 host-approved experiences around Cape Town / the Winelands / Overberg** with different budgets and styles. Use the CSV as the shared source of truth. Each record needs host permission, unique story, real logistics, transport needs, current pricing, verified date, photography rights and an emergency/backup plan. Avoid publishing fragile or private locations without permission.

A useful weekly format is **The Wednesday Drop**: one verified story, the human behind it, who it's for, practical details and a real way to enquire or book. One more ready-to-use weekend pairing may be shared as a bonus. Never invent live availability, scarcity or exact travel times.

## Next technical milestone
The existing `/api/discover` is an idea generator, not a factual venue matcher. Next, add persistent curated inventory to a database or authenticated sheet and update AI to choose ONLY among vetted published records; return cards with their IDs, actual availability and sources. If there is no appropriate match, collect a request for editorial follow-up. Add review/moderation and real email subscriptions with opt-in and unsubscribe handling.

## Responsible scarcity
FOMO is the curiosity of discovering something great, not fake countdowns or fake reservations. Only show "last seats", "this weekend only" and similar claims when confirmed by the provider. Protect sensitive natural or cultural sites.

## Test and iterate
Pilot 4 real drops with 20–30 interested locals before expanding nationally. Measure actual signups, opens, click-through, completed enquiries, confirmed visits and host satisfaction. Distinguish interest from actual bookings.
